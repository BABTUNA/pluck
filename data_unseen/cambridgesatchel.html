Care Instructions

Keep Away from Heat & Sunlight

Protect your bag from prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or heat to prevent fading or drying.

Clean Regularly

Wipe your bag with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust and dirt.

Use a Dust Bag

Store your bag in its Cambridge Satchel dust bag when not in use to protect it from scratches and dust.

Condition Leather

Apply leather conditioner periodically to maintain suppleness and prevent drying.

Handle with Care

Avoid contact with wet sanitizer as it can damage the leather's surface.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight when not in use to preserve its color and texture.
