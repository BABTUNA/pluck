Buyer’s Notes

Inspired by classic 1950’s design, our stylish flat rattan occasional chair has a strong black iron frame and blonde rattan finish.

Our contemporary twist on this classic Scandinavian design has been especially designed for your comfort with a cosy, rounded seat.

The perfect occasional chair for your lounge or garden room, pair with our Sumptuous Sheepskin Single Rug - Ivory 

Size & Info

W 84 x H 83 x D 72cm

Weight 7.8kg

Made from woven natural rattan with an iron frame

As this item has been made from natural rattan, the tonal blonde colour will vary slightly from chair to chair

Delivery & Returns

One Man Delivery

Our One Man Delivery service costs £35 and takes 3–5 working days.

You will be contacted within 2 working days of your order to arrange a delivery slot. Please tell us then of any access issues that may hinder delivery.

For more information visit our delivery information page.

Returns and Refunds

Most items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided they are in original, unused, saleable condition and repacked in the original packaging. We are only able to cover the cost of returns for faulty, damaged or wrongly dispatched items.

To make a return, or for more information, please complete the form on our returns page.
