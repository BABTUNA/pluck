    Product information "Aerosoft Aircraft CRJ V2"

    One of the first airliner add-ons for Microsoft Flight Simulator, the Aerosoft CRJ is now available as version 2 and compatible with MSFS 2024! Featuring a fully remodelled cabin, a revised flight model, full Navigraph Charts implementation and much more, v2 expands on the original aircraft with new features, bug fixes and expanded customization.

    A new cabin model has been created for each of the four aircraft variants with full cabin and galley lighting. Small details such as lights for the emergency, exit, seat belt and no smoking signs and optional pilot and copilot avatars enhance the immersion even further.
    Plan your flight with Simbrief import**, including weather information and an OFP viewer, as well as Navigraph Terminal and Enroute Charts with live updates for the navigation database.*

    A new flight model based on MSFS’ new aerodynamics provides a more realistic behavior, closely reflecting the performance of the real aircraft. Automated GSX door handling and refueling support***, weather radar integration and a landing distance calculator add further depths to the simulation of this popular regional jet.

    Finally, extensive customization options for knob acceleration, sounds, autopilot configuration, throttle calibration and more allow you to configure the aircraft to your liking.

    Modeling:

    • Included are the CRJ-550/-700/-900/-1000 models with 31 liveries
    • Highly detailed modeling based on many photos of the original aircraft
    • Covered with high resolution PBR textures
    • Fluid animations of moving parts such as landing gear, flaps and doors
    • New cabin model for each of the four aircraft variants
    • Full cabin and galley lighting
    • Emergency and Exit lights
    • Seat belt and no smoking lights
    • Optional Pilot and Copilot avatars
    • Separately opening forward and center cargo doors on CRJ 900 and 1000

    Flight Model:

    • Revised flight model is based on Microsoft Flight Simulator's new aerodynamic model to provide more realistic behavior of the CRJ in all flight conditions. It has been fine tuned to reflect the performance and reactive behavior of the CRJ

    Aircraft Systems:

    • Flight Management System and Rockwell Collins Proline 4 Avionics including Advisory and Coupled Vertical Navigation
    • Delivered with Navigraph September 2023 (AIRAC 2309) navigation database
    • Head-up Guidance System
    • Refuel/Defuel Panel in the cockpit
    • Realistic simulation of the aircraft systems
    • Weather Radar
    • Terrain Radar

    Electronic Flight Bag:

    • Detailed performance calculations based on actual weight and balance and graphical display of actualized load sheet
    • Transfer of weight, fuel and calculated V-speeds into the FMS
    • Checklists for all flight situations
    • Individual configuration settings for aircraft maintenance, preferred aircraft state when loading
    • Navigraph Airport Charts display with pinned charts functionality*
    • Navigraph Enroute Charts display with flight plan overlay*
    • Simbrief flight plan import, including optional flight plan transfer to FMS and aircraft payload and fuel loading**
    • Simbrief OFP Viewer**
    • Landing Distance Calculator
    • Many options to configure the aircraft to your liking (knob acceleration, GSX support, sound options, autopilot configuration, throttle lever calibration and much more)

    General features:

    • Eight manuals, including detailed step-by-step documentation
    • Immersive Wwise soundset, including (optional) passenger announcements, flightdeck immersion effects, landing gear sounds (bumps) and door sounds – all recorded from the real aircraft
    • Compatible with all MSFS-compatible controllers, including Honeycomb & Thrustmaster Airbus TCA controllers
    • Many new options to tweak the product to your liking and your hardware
    • Extensive GSX Pro support (doors handling and aircraft refueling)***

    Version 2 of the CRJ has been extensively reworked and updated with 190 changes. For details, please refer to the change log.

    *) requires an active Navigraph Unlimited subscription
    **) requires a Simbrief account (free)
    ***) requires FSDreamteam GSX Pro. This product is available separately at www.fsdreamteam.com/products_gsxpro.html
