Highlights Endurace CF 7 Di2

The inside line on Endurace CF

Built to ride your way, Endurace CF opens new opportunities to experience more of the beauty cycling has to offer. All-roads capability in a pure, yet versatile package, watch the inside line on what makes Endurace CF stand out.

Discover cycling with Sophie Alisch

Former boxer-turned-pro-cyclist Sophie Alisch didn’t follow a traditional path into the sport. Like the rest of us though, she found joy in the freedom and ease we experience in the saddle. What’s the Endurace CF made for? She’s here to talk us through it.

Test ride an Endurace CF near you

Visit us at our flagship showrooms, authorised partner stores or at an experience centre near you for your chance to get up-close and ride the new Endurace CF. You’ll also find locations for buying and fitting advice, as well as all your service needs.
Here’s where you can find us.

Mechanic’s POV

Take a closer look at the Canyon Endurace CF, uncovering the practical design details, clever engineering and race-ready features that make it one of Canyon’s most versatile road bikes.

Purity meets versatility

The Endurace CF is focused on delivering pure joy and confidence on any ride, bringing modern style, progressive features and carbon performance to new levels of affordability. Intuitive and easy to use, options like a strapless frame bag enhance practicality while keeping things clean. All this on a frame that weighs just 950 g.

Clear cockpit. Clean lines.

Semi-integrated cable routing on Endurace CF means your bike always cuts the freshest figure, plus makes it easier to attach a front roll for bikepacking or burrito bag for extra snacks. Lines routed beneath the cockpit also make for simple bar swaps if required.
Shop DEFEND Fast Fenders here.

38 mm clearance: Ready for any road

Both frame and fork clear 38 mm rubber, giving you freedom to fit tyres best suited to where you ride most. Endurace CF ships with 32 mm tyres to balance fast rolling and comfort. Our DEFEND Fast Fenders can be installed in seconds and clear up to 35 mm tyres, transforming Endurace CF into the bike for all seasons.

Comfort and control: Ergonomic bars

Endurace CF has a targeted ergonomic handlebar design that minimises pressure points. Its compact form, subtle backsweep and flare makes it more comfortable to maintain positions for longer and easier to switch up your grip, whether you’re cruising on the tops or getting low on the drops.

Iconic comfort: VCLS 2.0

Our new-look SP0100 VCLS 2.0 seatpost delivers industry-leading comfort in a simple, lightweight package, and now has a direct interface for a rear light. Allowing up to 20 mm flex, this seatpost delivers a magic ride quality, protecting your body from shocks and vibrations over the roughest surfaces.

All-day fit: Sport Geometry

Our endurance-focused Sport Geometry enables an upright fit for all-day comfort. Shorter cranks (165 mm now standard on size M) also reduce joint stress and make it easier to spin a higher cadence. Updated handling and a longer wheelbase perfectly balance race-inspired agility with confidence-boosting stability - this is our best-handling Endurace yet.

Bike classification

Bikes of this category are designed for riding on hard-surface roads where the wheels remain in permanent contact to the ground. These are in general road racing bicycles with racing handlebars or straight handlebars, triathlon or time trial bicycles. The permissible maximum overall weight comprising rider, luggage and bicycle should not exceed 120 kg. Under certain circumstances this permissible maximum weight can be further limited by the component manufacturers’ recommendations for use. Proven cyclocross and gravel bikes with racing handlebars and cantilever or disc brakes are a special case in this category. In addition, these bikes are also suitable for gravel paths and off-road trails where a short loss of tyre contact with the ground due to small stairs or steps at a height of 15 to 20 cm can occur.
